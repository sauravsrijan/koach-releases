Koach

Hear how you really come across in your calls.

Koach records your calls on your Mac, transcribes them, and coaches you on how you communicate: how much you talk, the questions you ask, your pace, filler words, and what you promised to do.

Download for Mac
macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

What it does

  • Records both sidesYour mic and the call's audio, from Zoom, Meet, Slack or anything else.
  • Coaching after every callA summary, how your tone came across, and specific suggestions.
  • Your numbers over timeTalk ratio, questions, pace and filler words, with trends.
  • Action itemsWho promised what, with priorities and deadlines, in one list.

Install

  1. Open the download and drag Koach to Applications.
  2. Allow it to open the first time. Koach isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open Koach, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or run this once in Terminal: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Koach.app
  3. Follow the setup. Your name, Microphone and Screen Recording permission, and your own API keys: an AssemblyAI key for transcripts, plus one of Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter for coaching.

Private by design

Recordings, transcripts and coaching are stored encrypted on your Mac. Audio goes straight from your Mac to AssemblyAI using your key, and transcripts to the coaching provider you pick. Nothing passes through Koach's servers. Koach updates itself and asks before restarting.
